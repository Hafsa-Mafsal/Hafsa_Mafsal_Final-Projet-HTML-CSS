Support families displaced by the Myanmar military

Since the Myanmar military seized control of the country in February 2021, nearly 700,000 people have been forced from their homes by the resulting violence. That brings the total number of people displaced right now to a record 1 million people. Almost half of them are children. We were there then. And we're here now to support them.

We're still supporting schools in conflict areas, training local health workers and investing in communities with the hope that one day peace will arrive. Children in Myanmar shouldn't suffer while the adults are fighting.
